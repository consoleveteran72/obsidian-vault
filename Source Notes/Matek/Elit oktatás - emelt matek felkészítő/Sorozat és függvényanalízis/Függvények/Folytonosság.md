
**Def. (Cauchy-féle / $\varepsilon$-$\delta$ definíció):**
$f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, $x_0 \in D_f$, $f$ **folytonos $x_0$-ban**, ha:

$$\forall \varepsilon > 0\text{-hoz } \exists \delta > 0, \text{ hogy ha } |x - x_0| < \delta \implies |f(x) - f(x_0)| < \varepsilon$$

---

**Def.:** $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, $x_0 \in D_f$, $f$ **folytonos $x_0$-ban**, ha:

1. $\exists f(x_0) \iff x_0 \in D_f$
2. $\exists \lim_{x \to x_0} f(x) \text{ határérték } = A$
3. $A = \lim_{x \to x_0} f(x) = f(x_0)$
